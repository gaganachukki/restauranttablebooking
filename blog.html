TABLE PROTOCOL

The Modern Dining Etiquette Handbook

Effortless guidelines to ensure your dining experience is gracious and memorable for both guests and staff.

01

The 15-Minute Courtesy

If running late, notify the venue via your dashboard or text. Restaurants hold tables for 15 minutes before releasing to waiting parties.

02

Dietary Transparency

Always communicate allergies during booking, not when plates arrive at the table. This allows the kitchen to prepare allergen-free prep surfaces.

03

Discreet Photography

Capturing your beautiful dish is celebrated, but avoid flash photography and standing over tables in fine dining spaces to respect fellow diners.

04

Course Coordination

During tasting menus, try to eat dishes when warm to savor the intended textures. The kitchen orchestrates subsequent courses to your table's pace.
