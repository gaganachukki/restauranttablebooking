OUR GENESIS

From Frustrated Foodies to
Hospitality Innovators.

It started on a crowded Saturday evening in Chennai. Our founding team had spent over forty minutes phoning five renowned restaurants, navigating busy signals, uncertain scribbled waitlists, and noisy background chatter—only to be told on arrival that their table was given away to a walk-in party.

We realized that while food delivery had advanced by leaps and bounds, the physical act of gathering at a restaurant remained grounded in outdated pen-and-paper friction. STACKLY was created to restore elegance, respect, and technological reliability to table reservations.

“We don’t treat dining as an algorithmic transaction. Every reservation is a promise between a guest looking forward to an evening and a kitchen preparing to welcome them.”

— Vikram Sengupta, Culinary Director & Co-Founder

OUR PILLARS

The Principles We Stand By

Six foundational values that guide our product choices, restaurant partnerships, and community relationships.

Zero Surcharges

We believe table reservations should never come with hidden convenience fees or markups. Dining at a STACKLY venue costs exactly the menu price.

Personal Curation

We do not scrape or list every restaurant blindly. We celebrate dining spots with distinct culinary voices, fresh ingredients, and exceptional service.

Guaranteed Seating

Confirmed bookings mean your table is reserved and prepped. If a wait occurs over 15 minutes, our host concierge team assists immediately.

Kitchen Respect

We empower restaurant hosts and culinary teams with intelligent tools that respect kitchen capacity, prevent overbooking, and minimize food waste.

Verified Gastronomy

Every culinary partner is thoroughly audited for ingredient freshness, hygiene rigor, and authentic taste profiles before joining STACKLY.

Bespoke Hospitality

From dietary requirements to celebratory arrangements, our concierge team works directly with floor managers to tailor your table.
