Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guidance on reservation policies, arrival protocols, and special requests.

How do I modify or cancel an existing reservation?
You can modify or cancel any active reservation directly from the "My Reservations" tab up to 2 hours before your scheduled dining time. Simply click the "Cancel" button or reach out to our concierge via Live Chat for party size adjustments.
What is the grace period if my party is running late?
Our partnered establishments hold reserved tables for 15 minutes beyond your scheduled seating time. If you anticipate arriving past this window, please notify the Maître d' through our Concierge Live Chat to safeguard your table.
Is there a dress code requirement at fine dining partners?
Most of our fine dining and rooftop venues observe an elegant smart-casual attire guideline. Collared shirts and closed footwear are recommended for evening seatings. Athletic wear and beachwear are restricted.
How does STACKLY handle severe dietary allergies?
When you input your dietary restrictions in your Profile Settings or during booking, our platform automatically transmits your allergen alerts directly to the restaurant's executive kitchen team prior to your arrival.
Is valet parking complimentary with my booking?
Yes, all premier and five-star partnered venues featured on STACKLY offer complimentary valet parking upon presenting your digital STACKLY booking confirmation.
